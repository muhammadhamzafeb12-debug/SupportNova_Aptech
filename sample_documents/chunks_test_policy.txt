NexaLink Network Outage Credit Policy 2026.
Section 1: Eligibility. Customers experiencing outages over 4 hours qualify for credits.
Section 2: Credit Amount. Ten percent of daily bill for each additional hour.
Section 3: Approval. Agents may approve up to fifty dollars automatically.
Section 4: Appeals. Submit appeals within 30 days to the billing department.
Section 5: Regulatory Compliance. FCC regulations apply to all credit determinations.

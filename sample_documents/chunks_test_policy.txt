VelvoCart Delivery Policy 2026.
Section 1: Eligibility. Customers experiencing delivery delays qualify for credits.
Section 2: Credit Amount. Ten percent of order value for each additional day.
Section 3: Approval. Agents may approve up to fifty dollars automatically.
Section 4: Appeals. Submit appeals within 30 days to the fulfillment department.
Section 5: Regulatory Compliance. Consumer protection laws apply to all determinations.

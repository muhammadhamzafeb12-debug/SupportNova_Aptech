NexaLink Communications — Customer Refund & Credit Policy 2026

1. SCOPE AND ELIGIBILITY
This policy governs all credit and refund requests submitted to NexaLink Communications
by residential and commercial customers. All eligible customers who experience documented 
service interruptions, billing errors, or equipment malfunctions are entitled to apply for 
credits, pro-rated refunds, or service adjustments under the terms described herein.

2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Customers are eligible for refunds or bill credits in the following circumstances:
- Service outages exceeding four (4) cumulative hours within any 30-day billing cycle.
- Billing errors such as duplicate charges, incorrect rate application, or unauthorized fees.
- Failure of leased or purchased equipment during normal operating conditions.
- Misapplication of promotional discounts that were actively contracted.
- Damaged product returns where the product failure was due to manufacturing defect.

For physical product damage or damaged product return, the customer must submit a return 
merchandise authorization (RMA) code within 30 days of identifying the defect. Refund 
eligibility for damaged product cases is 100% of the purchase price if reported within 7 days,
50% if reported within 8–30 days.

3. CREDIT AUTHORIZATION LEVELS
The following authorization matrix governs who may approve bill credits:
- Credits up to $50: Customer Service Agent (Tier 1)
- Credits $51–$150: Service Manager or Supervisor
- Credits $151–$500: Department Director
- Credits above $500: Chief Customer Officer approval required

4. OUTAGE SLA CREDIT SCHEDULE
Network outage lasting 0–4 hours: No automatic credit.
Network outage lasting 4–24 hours: 10% of daily charge per hour beyond 4 hours.
Network outage lasting more than 24 hours: 100% daily charge credit.
All outage credits are applied within 1–2 billing cycles.

5. RETURN POLICY FOR EQUIPMENT
Customers may return leased equipment within 30 days of service discontinuation
without penalty. Return shipping labels are provided free of charge. Failure to 
return equipment within 60 days results in a non-return fee per the Equipment Agreement.

6. CLAIMS AND APPEALS PROCESS
Customers must submit refund claims within 90 days of the incident date.
Appeals to denied claims must be submitted in writing within 30 days of denial.
All refund decisions are final once the 30-day appeal window closes.

7. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
All refund practices are governed by applicable state and federal telecommunications
regulations including FCC Part 8 Open Internet Rules and applicable state PUC guidelines.
Non-compliance escalations are handled by the Legal & Compliance department.

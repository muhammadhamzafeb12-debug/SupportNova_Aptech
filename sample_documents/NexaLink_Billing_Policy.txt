NexaLink Communications — Billing & Credit Standard Operating Procedure

SECTION 1: PURPOSE
This SOP defines the step-by-step procedures for Customer Service Agents and Billing
Specialists to resolve customer billing disputes, credit requests, and payment adjustments
for NexaLink residential and business accounts.

SECTION 2: BILLING DISPUTE INTAKE
Step 1: Verify the customer's identity using Name, Account Number, and Last 4 digits 
of the Social Security Number or Tax ID for business accounts.
Step 2: Pull the customer's account in the Billing Management System (BMS).
Step 3: Document the disputed charges including charge date, amount, and type.
Step 4: Cross-reference the disputed amount against the current rate card and promotional 
agreement on file.

SECTION 3: AUTO-PAY DISCREPANCY RESOLUTION
If the customer reports an auto-pay amount discrepancy:
- Check autopay enrollment status in the BSCS payment system.
- Verify the bank account or card on file has not expired.
- Apply a one-time paper bill fee waiver (up to $10) if the disruption was due to a 
  system error.

SECTION 4: CREDIT ISSUANCE PROCEDURE
After confirming the billing error:
1. Enter the adjustment code corresponding to the error type in BMS.
2. Apply the credit to the next billing cycle unless customer requests immediate reversal.
3. Document the action in the case management system with agent ID and timestamp.
4. Notify the customer via email and/or SMS confirmation.

SECTION 5: ESCALATION RULES
Escalate to Supervisor if:
- Credit authorization exceeds $150.
- Customer threatens regulatory action (FCC, BBB, State AG).
- Customer account is flagged as a VIP or enterprise account.
- Agent has been unable to resolve after two prior interactions.

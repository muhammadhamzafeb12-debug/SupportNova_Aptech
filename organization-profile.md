## 🏢 Business Overview

VelvoCart is a leading enterprise e-commerce platform and online marketplace providing consumers and businesses with direct access to over 15 million catalog products across Consumer Electronics, Fashion & Apparel, Home & Kitchen, Pantry Groceries, and Verified 3rd-Party Marketplace Sellers.

### Product & Service Catalog (12 Offerings)

1. **VelvoCart Prime:** Annual/monthly membership providing free same-day shipping, exclusive deals, and member perks.
2. **Electronics Store:** Consumer gadgets, smartphones, laptops, 4K TVs, and smart home systems.
3. **Fashion & Apparel:** Clothing, footwear, activewear, luxury accessories, and jewelry.
4. **Home & Kitchen:** Furniture, cookware, bedding, home decor, and major appliances.
5. **Grocery Delivery:** Fresh produce, cold perishables, and pantry staples delivered in 2 hours.
6. **VelvoCart Marketplace:** Platform hosting over 50,000 independent verified sellers.
7. **Digital Gift Cards:** Digital gift cards and store credit vouchers.
8. **VelvoPay Digital Wallet:** 1-click checkout, cashback balance, and buy-now-pay-later installment plans.
9. **Same-Day Express Delivery:** Dedicated urban logistics network for rapid delivery.
10. **VelvoCart Returns Center:** Reverse logistics drop-off locations at 5,000+ retail hubs.
11. **Customer Protection Plan:** Extended warranty and accidental damage insurance.
12. **Bulk & Business Orders:** Wholesale supply catalog and tax-exempt B2B corporate accounts.
